    Questions we hear a lot

    How much do inspection and maintenance cost?

    Our prices follow the market, and the labor fee is fixed for each service. The technician tells you the full cost after the inspection and before starting, and the decision is yours.

    Do you serve homes or only businesses?

    Both. We repair a bedroom AC in an apartment with the same care we give a restaurant cold room.

    Which areas do you cover?

    Makkah and its neighborhoods. If you are outside Makkah, message us and we will tell you if we can reach you.

    What are your working hours?

    Saturday to Thursday, 9:30 AM to 12 PM and 4:30 PM to 11 PM. Friday, 4:30 PM to 11 PM.

    What types of equipment do you repair?

    Split, window, central and package AC units, home and display refrigerators, cold rooms and freezer rooms, ice machines, and commercial kitchen equipment.

    Who are the technicians entering my home?

    The Cold West team itself, under the supervision of a Saudi refrigeration specialist. We introduce the technician to you before arrival.
